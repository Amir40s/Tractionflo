# Complete MVP Flow

## 1. Monitor Engagement

Capture:

- Comments
- Story replies
- DMs
- Mentions
- New followers

## 2. AI Starts Conversation

Example prompts:

- "Thanks for engaging with my content. What brought you here?"
- "I noticed you've been following my content. What are you currently working on?"

## 3. AI Answers Questions

Creator uploads:

- FAQs
- Product information
- Pricing
- Course details
- Services
- Brand information

AI answers automatically.

Example questions:

- "How much is your course?"
- "Do you offer coaching?"
- "How do I join?"
- "What's included?"

## 4. AI Qualifies

Learns:

- Budget
- Goals
- Interest level
- Timeline

## 5. AI Upsells

Based on conversation:

- Cold: follow creator
- Warm: join newsletter/community
- Hot: book call, buy course, or purchase service

## 6. Human Escalation

When AI detects:

- Complex question
- Complaint
- Refund request
- Partnership opportunity
- Brand deal
- High-ticket lead

It creates an alert.

Email sent to creator:

Subject: High-Priority Conversation Needs Attention

Summary:

- User profile
- Conversation summary
- Reason for escalation
- Direct reply link

## 7. Creator Takes Over

Creator clicks:

- Take over conversation
- Reply manually

AI stops responding.

## 8. Analytics

Show:

- Conversations started
- Questions answered
- Followers converted
- Leads generated
- Sales generated
- Escalations
