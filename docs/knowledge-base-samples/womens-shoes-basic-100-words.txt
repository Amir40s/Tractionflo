Women's Shoes Basic Knowledge

We sell women's shoes for daily wear, office, parties, weddings, travel, and gifting. Main styles include flats, sandals, heels, wedges, pumps, loafers, sneakers, boots, slides, and mules. Ask customers for shoe size, foot length, width, preferred color, occasion, budget, and comfort needs. For long events, suggest block heels, wedges, cushioned flats, or padded sandals. For wide feet, suggest round toes, soft straps, loafers, or sneakers. Confirm stock, price, size, material, and delivery date before promising anything. Size exchange may be possible only for unworn shoes in original box. Always guide buyers politely and keep recommendations simple.
