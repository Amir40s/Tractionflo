Luna & Lattice Women's Jewellery Knowledge Base - Manual FAQ Paste Text

FAQ 1
Question: Will the jewellery color fade?
Answer: Plated and fashion jewellery can fade over time if exposed to water, perfume, sweat, or chemicals. To preserve shine, keep it dry, wipe after use, and store it separately in a pouch or box.

FAQ 2
Question: Do you have jewellery for sensitive skin?
Answer: Some items may be better for sensitive skin, such as stainless steel, sterling silver, or hypoallergenic-labelled pieces. Please share the product you like so we can confirm the material before you order.

FAQ 3
Question: Can I get gift packaging?
Answer: Yes, gift packaging is available for selected pieces. Share the product code and occasion, and we can confirm box options, pricing, and availability.

FAQ 4
Question: How do I choose jewellery for a wedding outfit?
Answer: Share your outfit color, neckline, hairstyle, event type, and budget. We can suggest matching earrings, necklace sets, bangles, tikka sets, or statement pieces based on your look.

FAQ 5
Question: Can I return earrings?
Answer: For hygiene reasons, earrings and pierced jewellery may not be returnable unless defective. If there is a defect, please share your order number, clear photos or unboxing video, and delivery date for review.
