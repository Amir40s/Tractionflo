Knowledge Base Test File

Business overview
PadelPoint is a premium padel court booking business with indoor and outdoor courts. Customers book courts for casual games, coaching, tournaments, birthday events, corporate wellness sessions, and beginner padel lessons. The venue is located at DHA Phase 6, Lahore, near Raya Commercial. Bookings are handled through Instagram DM, WhatsApp, phone call, and website booking form.

Assistant behavior
When a customer asks to book a court, ask for date, time, number of players, and whether they need rackets or coaching. Do not confirm final availability until the booking calendar is checked. If a question is not covered in this file, say the team can confirm it manually.

Cancellation and reschedule policy
Cancel 18 hours or more before booking: full advance can be moved to another available slot.
Cancel 8 to 18 hours before booking: 50 percent of advance can be moved.
Cancel less than 8 hours before booking: advance is non-refundable.
Coach cancellation less than 8 hours before the session is charged at 50 percent of coaching fee.
Outdoor rain policy: if rain makes the outdoor court unplayable, the booking is moved to a new available slot or shifted to an indoor court if available. Indoor court price difference may apply.

Common customer questions and answers
Question: How much is indoor padel on Saturday evening?
Answer: Weekend indoor court booking is PKR 11,000 per hour.

Question: Do you provide rackets?
Answer: Rackets are available for rent at PKR 700 per racket per session. Court booking does not include rackets by default.

Question: Can beginners play?
Answer: Yes. Beginners are welcome. PadelPoint also offers beginner private coaching and group coaching.

Question: Do you have ladies-only coaching?
Answer: Yes. Ladies-only coaching slots are available Tuesday and Thursday from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Question: What if it rains?
Answer: Outdoor bookings can be moved to another slot or shifted to an indoor court if available. Indoor court price difference may apply.

Question: Can we book for a corporate event?
Answer: Yes. The Corporate Wellness Session includes 3 hours, two courts, coach introduction, water, and tournament support for PKR 95,000.

Question: Is this a cricket ground?
Answer: No. PadelPoint is only for padel court bookings, padel coaching, memberships, and padel events.
