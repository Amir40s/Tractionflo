GreenWicket Cricket Ground Booking System
Knowledge Base Test File

Business overview
GreenWicket Cricket Ground is a private cricket ground for box cricket, tape-ball cricket, hard-ball net practice, corporate matches, school tournaments, and weekend league games. The ground is located near Main Canal Road, Lahore, 8 minutes from Expo Centre. The business handles bookings through WhatsApp, Instagram DM, phone call, and website form.

Assistant behavior
When a customer asks about booking, first ask for date, preferred time slot, match type, and number of players. Do not confirm availability until the booking calendar is checked. If the customer asks something not covered in this file, say that the team can confirm it manually instead of inventing details.

Booking deposit
Every booking requires a 30 percent advance payment.
Corporate events require 50 percent advance payment.
The remaining balance must be paid before play starts.
Payment methods: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer, and cash at reception.

Cancellation and reschedule policy
Cancel 24 hours or more before the booking: full advance can be moved to another date.
Cancel 12 to 24 hours before the booking: 50 percent of the advance can be moved.
Cancel less than 12 hours before the booking: advance is non-refundable.
Rain policy: if rain makes the ground unplayable, the booking is moved to a new available slot at no extra charge.

Common customer questions and answers
Question: Do you have night cricket?
Answer: Yes. Night cricket is available with floodlights from 8:00 PM to 12:00 AM on weekdays and until 1:00 AM on weekends.

Question: How much is the main ground on Sunday night?
Answer: Weekend main ground slots are PKR 14,000 per hour. Night slots include floodlights.

Question: Can we book for a corporate match?
Answer: Yes. Corporate match packages start from PKR 28,000. Please share your date, time, number of players, and whether you need umpire, photography, or refreshments.

Question: Is advance payment required?
Answer: Yes. Standard bookings require 30 percent advance payment. Corporate events require 50 percent advance payment.

Question: What happens if it rains?
Answer: If rain makes the ground unplayable, the booking is moved to a new available slot at no extra charge.

Question: Do you provide bats and balls?
Answer: Stumps are included. Tape balls and tennis balls are available for purchase. Bats can be rented for PKR 500 per match.

Question: Do you have football or padel?
Answer: No. GreenWicket is only for cricket bookings, practice nets, and cricket events.
